# PersonaGraph Simulation Prompts for Ralph Lauren Marketing

## Prompt Option 1: Gen Z Brand Perception
> Simulate how Gen Z consumers (ages 18-27) on Twitter and Reddit react to Ralph Lauren's attempt to position itself as culturally relevant to younger audiences. Include fashion influencers, streetwear enthusiasts, sustainability-conscious consumers, luxury brand competitors (Tommy Hilfiger, Burberry), and mainstream fashion media. Predict whether the "Ralph Lauren Christmas" viral aesthetic trend can translate into sustained brand loyalty, or if it remains a fleeting social media moment. Analyze sentiment around pricing ($150-300 polo shirts vs. fast fashion alternatives), brand authenticity, and sustainability criticism.

## Prompt Option 2: New Product Launch Campaign
> Predict social media response to Ralph Lauren launching a new "Polo Earth" sustainable fashion collection made from 100% recycled materials, priced 20% higher than standard Polo Ralph Lauren. Simulate reactions from eco-conscious consumers, fashion critics, brand loyalists, sustainability skeptics, Gen Z activists, competitor brands, and financial analysts. Focus on whether the sustainability premium is accepted or criticized as greenwashing, given Ralph Lauren's "Not Good Enough" sustainability rating from Good On You and allegations of supply chain labor issues.

## Prompt Option 3: Digital Marketing Strategy
> Simulate public opinion dynamics around Ralph Lauren's aggressive shift to direct-to-consumer digital sales and reduction of off-price/discount channel presence. Include perspectives from: bargain shoppers who previously bought Ralph Lauren at TJ Maxx and Nordstrom Rack, aspiring luxury consumers, brand purists who welcome exclusivity, retail industry analysts, competitor brand strategists, and social media influencers. Predict whether the brand elevation strategy strengthens or erodes the customer base on Twitter and Reddit.

## Prompt Option 4: China Market Expansion
> Predict social media reactions (both Western and simulated Chinese social media dynamics) to Ralph Lauren's aggressive expansion into Tier 1 and Tier 2 Chinese cities with new flagship stores and WeChat commerce. Simulate perspectives from Chinese luxury consumers, domestic brand advocates (Li-Ning, Anta), Western market analysts, geopolitical commentators, sustainability critics, and fashion journalists. Analyze whether American heritage branding resonates or faces backlash in the current geopolitical climate.

## Prompt Option 5: Olympic Partnership Campaign
> Simulate social media reaction to Ralph Lauren unveiling the Team USA parade uniforms for the upcoming Olympic Games. Include patriotic consumers, fashion critics, competing sportswear brands (Nike, Adidas, Under Armour), sustainability advocates questioning textile sourcing, international fashion media, and athletes as brand ambassadors. Predict viral potential, sentiment split, and whether the Olympic moment converts to sustained brand commercial impact.
